# Focused competitor audit
Checked 2026-10-10. This is evidence of feature overlap, not a uniqueness or market validation claim.

| Product | Observed capability | Overlap / distinction to test | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Letterboxd | Diary, half-star ratings, reviews, lists, follows, watchlist | Foundation overlaps intentionally; no evidence here establishes absence of the full USP workflows | https://letterboxd.com/ | High |
| Letterboxd | Imports dated viewings, reviews, rewatch flags | Preserve viewing events independently of current opinion | https://letterboxd.com/importing-data/ | High |
| Trakt | Personal notes on history, collection and ratings | Personal notes overlap Memory Vault; structured anchors plus private fuzzy recall are the workflow hypothesis | https://forums.trakt.tv/t/personal-notes/16308 | High |
| SIMKL | Ratings history with search/management | Timeline is not claimed as novel rating history; distinguish explicit append-only reflection without rewatch and side-by-side reasons | https://docs.simkl.org/how-to-use-simkl/core-features/content-tracking/tracking-content/watch-history/ratings-history-page | High |
| Trakt | Public requests for richer ratings filtering | Discoverability of earlier opinions is a pain hypothesis | https://roadmap.trakt.tv/p/full-featured-ratings-page | Medium |
| SIMKL | Viewing history, recommendations, social discovery | Foundation overlap | https://simkl.com/ | High |
